# Travel Delay Cover — Frequently Asked Questions

These answers explain the Sample Travel Insurance Policy (fictional, for the ClausePath demo). Clause numbers in brackets point to the policy. The insurer decides every claim [CL-5].

## Is my situation covered?

**My flight was cancelled because of a typhoon. Am I covered?**
Weather, including typhoons, is a covered cause [TD-2], and a cancellation counts as a travel delay [DEF-5]. You need Plan Plus or Plan Premier [PLAN-1], and you must have bought the policy before the typhoon warning was announced [EX-1].

**My flight was two hours late. Can I claim?**
Not under this cover. A delay counts only when the flight leaves at least 4 hours late or is cancelled [DEF-5].

**I bought the policy the day the typhoon warning came out. Does that matter?**
It can. Causes announced or forecast before you bought the policy are excluded [EX-1]. If it was the same day, the insurer checks the exact times, so keep the purchase confirmation.

**I was late to the gate and missed my flight. Is the hotel covered?**
No. Missing a flight because you arrived late is excluded [EX-2].

**I extended my trip without changing my policy. My later flight was cancelled.**
Delays outside the period of insurance are not covered, including days added without amending the policy [EX-3].

**My flight home was cancelled on the last day of my policy. Do I need to extend my insurance?**
No. When a covered delay keeps you from getting home, your cover extends automatically until your replacement flight leaves, for up to 7 days, at no extra cost and without telling us [TD-6]. Hotel, meals and transport during those days are paid with the same daily and per-trip limits [PLAN-2]. If you choose to stay longer than the replacement flight you could take, the extra days are not covered [EX-3].

## What can I claim?

**What expenses are covered?**
Meals and non-alcoholic drinks, accommodation for each night you wait, local transport (including to another airport for your new flight), and calls or mobile data needed to rearrange travel [TD-3].

**What is not covered?**
Alcohol and minibar, sightseeing and shopping, upgrades, the new ticket or change fees, anything the airline already paid for, and costs after your replacement flight leaves [TD-4].

**How much can I claim?**
Plan Plus pays up to USD 150 a day and USD 500 a trip; Plan Premier up to USD 250 a day and USD 1,000 a trip [PLAN-2].

**The airline gave me a hotel voucher. Can I also claim my own hotel?**
Not for the same night. Costs the airline paid or agreed to pay are excluded [TD-4](d). Your own meals or transport outside the voucher can still be claimed.

**I paid in yen. How is it converted?**
At the exchange rate on the date you paid [TD-5].

## Documents

**What do I need to send?**
The airline's written confirmation of the cancellation or delay, your original ticket, the boarding pass for your replacement flight, itemised receipts, the claim form, and a copy of your photo ID [CL-1].

**I didn't get a letter at the airport. Is it too late?**
No. Ask the airline's customer service by email after your trip [CL-2]. A forwarded cancellation email from the airline also works.

**My receipt photo is blurry.**
Retake it flat, in good light, with the date, amount and currency readable. Unreadable receipts cannot be checked.

**Should I send my bank details or passport by message?**
Never. Bank details and ID go only through the secure claims page [CL-1].

## Payment

**When will I get paid?**
Within 3 business days after all documents are received, or within 10 business days if the insurer needs to check something; they will tell you why and when [CL-4].

**How long do I have to claim?**
3 years from the date of the delay [CL-3].
