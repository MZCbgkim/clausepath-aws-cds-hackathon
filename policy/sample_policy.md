# Sample Travel Insurance Policy — Travel Delay Section

This is a fictional policy written for the ClausePath demo. It is not the wording of any real insurer. Clause IDs in square brackets are used for citation.

## Part A — Definitions

[DEF-1] **Insured Person**: the person named on the Policy Schedule.

[DEF-2] **Period of Insurance**: the trip dates shown on the Policy Schedule. Cover for a trip starts when the Insured Person leaves home for the trip and ends when they return home or at the end date on the Policy Schedule, whichever is earlier.

[DEF-3] **Scheduled Departure Time**: the departure time of the Insured Person's booked flight as shown on the ticket issued before the delay or cancellation.

[DEF-4] **Common Carrier**: an airline operating a scheduled passenger flight for which the Insured Person holds a confirmed ticket.

[DEF-5] **Travel Delay**: a flight on a Common Carrier that departs at least 4 hours after its Scheduled Departure Time, or is cancelled, for a reason listed in [TD-2].

[DEF-6] **Replacement Flight**: the first flight the Insured Person is rebooked on, or reasonably books, to continue the same journey after a Travel Delay.

[DEF-7] **Reasonable Additional Expenses**: expenses listed in [TD-3] that the Insured Person would not have had without the Travel Delay, at a cost comparable to what they would normally pay.

## Part B — Plans

[PLAN-1] The Travel Delay section applies only if the Policy Schedule shows **Plan Plus** or **Plan Premier**. **Plan Basic** does not include Travel Delay cover.

[PLAN-2] Benefit limits by plan:

| Plan | Per-day limit | Per-trip limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Basic | not covered | not covered |
| Plus | USD 150 | USD 500 |
| Premier | USD 250 | USD 1,000 |

## Part C — Travel Delay

[TD-1] **What we pay.** If the Insured Person's flight has a Travel Delay during the Period of Insurance, we pay Reasonable Additional Expenses incurred from the Scheduled Departure Time until the departure of the Replacement Flight, up to the limits in [PLAN-2].

[TD-2] **Covered causes.** Severe weather (including typhoons and storms), natural disaster, mechanical failure of the aircraft, closure of the airport or airspace by authorities, or a strike or industrial action by the airline's or airport's staff.

[TD-3] **Covered expense types.**
(a) meals and non-alcoholic drinks;
(b) hotel or other accommodation, for each night spent waiting for the Replacement Flight;
(c) local transport between the airport and the accommodation, or to another airport to take the Replacement Flight;
(d) phone calls and mobile data needed to rearrange travel.

[TD-4] **Expenses we do not pay under this section.**
(a) alcohol, minibar, entertainment, sightseeing, tours or shopping;
(b) upgrades to a higher class of travel or accommodation than the Insured Person originally booked;
(c) the cost of the Replacement Flight itself or any change fee (these may be covered under Trip Interruption, which is not part of this section);
(d) any amount the airline, a hotel, or another party has paid or refunded, or has agreed to pay;
(e) expenses incurred after the Replacement Flight departs, or before the Scheduled Departure Time.

[TD-5] **Currency.** Expenses in other currencies are converted to USD at the exchange rate on the date the expense was incurred.

[TD-6] **Automatic extension when the delay keeps you away.** If a flight has a Travel Delay during the Period of Insurance and, because of it, the Insured Person cannot return home by the end date, the Period of Insurance is extended automatically until the Replacement Flight departs, for up to 7 days after the end date. There is no extra premium and the Insured Person does not need to amend the Policy or tell us. Expenses during the extension are paid as in [TD-1], within the limits in [PLAN-2] (the per-trip limit covers the whole trip, including the extension). The extension ends if the Insured Person chooses to stay on after a Replacement Flight they could have taken.

## Part D — Exclusions

[EX-1] **Known events.** We do not pay if, when the Policy was bought, the cause of the delay had already been announced or forecast publicly — for example, a named storm warning for the departure area, an announced strike, or an announced airport closure.

[EX-2] **Insured Person's own actions.** We do not pay if the Insured Person missed the flight or the connection because they arrived late for check-in or boarding, did not hold valid travel documents, or chose to change to a different flight before any delay or cancellation was announced.

[EX-3] **Outside the Period of Insurance.** We do not pay for any delay that happens before the Period of Insurance starts or after it ends, including on days added to the trip by the Insured Person's own choice after the Policy was bought, unless the Policy Schedule was amended. Days added because of a Travel Delay are covered under [TD-6].

## Part E — How to claim

[CL-1] **Documents.** To claim for a Travel Delay, send us:
(a) written confirmation from the airline of the cancellation or delay, showing the flight number, date, length of delay and the reason;
(b) the original ticket or e-ticket showing the Scheduled Departure Time;
(c) the boarding pass or ticket for the Replacement Flight;
(d) itemised receipts for each expense, showing date, amount and currency;
(e) a completed claim form;
(f) a copy of the Insured Person's photo ID.
Bank details for payment are given only through our secure claims page, never by message or email.

[CL-2] **Getting the airline letter.** The airline's written confirmation can be requested at the airport counter or afterwards from the airline's customer service, usually by email.

[CL-3] **Time limit.** Claims must be sent within 3 years of the date of the Travel Delay.

[CL-4] **When we pay.** We pay within 3 business days after receiving all the documents in [CL-1]. If we need to investigate or verify the claim, we pay within 10 business days and tell the Insured Person the reason and the expected payment date. If payment is late without good reason, we add interest for the delay.

[CL-5] **Our decision.** We decide whether a claim is paid and how much after reviewing the documents. Information from our assistants, including automated assistants, is guidance and is not a decision on the claim.
